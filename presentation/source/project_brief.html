// The Objective

Build a transparent AI chatbot that relies on explicit rules rather than generative models. The goal was to master the "foundations of AI": input sanitization, intent classification, and deterministic state management.

Every response must be explainable, repeatable, and delivered through a continuous conversational loop.

// Scope & Requirements
  • Eight Intent GroupsGreetings, help, identity, time, date, joke, thanks, and farewell.
  • Continuous LifecycleLooping chat interface with clean exit commands (exit, quit, bye).
  • Input DisciplineNormalization of case, punctuation, and whitespace before matching.
  • Safe FallbacksGraceful handling of empty or unknown inputs without crashing.
